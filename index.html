Kingsfall

Tap a blue unit or town. Tap a marked hex to move; red means attack. Archers shoot 2 hexes, cavalry moves 3. Stack 3 units on one hex for a formation bonus (tap a stack again to switch units). Blue crystals boost nearby cities (+1 each); the central gold mine is worth 3x (+3). Merge 3 matching units on or beside each other into a bigger block (Company, then Battalion): they hit and defend much harder but only 1 block fits per hex. Capture every rival city to win.
